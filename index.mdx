Kallabot gives your business an AI agent that talks to customers the way your best receptionist would. It answers every call, day or night, in your customers' language. It books appointments, answers questions from your own price lists and pages, and hands over to a person when it should.

One agent can work everywhere your customers reach you:

## What you'll do in Kallabot

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Build your agent" icon="bot">
    Give it a name, a face, a voice and a personality. Tell it about your business in plain words. Add your price list, website or files so it answers with your facts. Connect your calendar so it can book. [Read more](/guides/agents)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Put it where your customers are" icon="radio-tower">
    Get a phone number, or keep your own and forward it. Connect WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger with your Meta login. Paste one line on your website for web chat. [Read more](/guides/phone-numbers)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Watch it work" icon="eye">
    Every call has a recording, a transcript and a short summary. Every chat sits in one inbox. Listen in on a live call, whisper the agent a tip, or take over yourself. [Read more](/guides/live-console)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Run it as a team" icon="users">
    Invite your staff, give each person the right role, organise them into branches and share out the minutes. [Read more](/guides/team)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
