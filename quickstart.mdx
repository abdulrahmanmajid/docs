You'll build a phone agent for your business and talk to it in your browser. You don't need a phone number for this; you can add one when you're happy with how it sounds.

**You need:** a Kallabot account ([sign up at kallabot.com](https://kallabot.com) with your name and email; we send you a six-digit code), a computer with a microphone, and ten minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new agent">
    Open **Agents** and click **Create agent ▾**, then **Voice agent**.

    Pick a template close to your business, like a clinic front desk or a property enquiry line, or start blank. A template only fills in a first draft. You can change everything.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Identity: who it is">
    - **Name.** What the agent calls itself, like "Sara".
    - **Face.** Pick one of the illustrated faces. It's how your team spots this agent in lists.
    - **Language.** The main language it speaks. For Arabic, open it to choose a dialect, such as Emirati or Egyptian.
    - **Personality.** Warm, Professional, Upbeat or Calm. Each one plays you a sample opening line.

    <Tip>You can also describe your business in a sentence and let Kallabot write the first draft for you.</Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Voice: how it sounds">
    Hover over a voice to hear it. Pick one that fits your business. You can come back and change it any time.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Instructions: what it does">
    Write to your agent the way you'd brief a new receptionist. Plain sentences work best:

    ```text
    You answer the phone for Bright Smile Dental in Dubai Marina.
    Help callers book a cleaning or a check-up.
    We're open 9am to 6pm, Saturday to Thursday.
    If someone is in pain, offer the first free slot today.
    Never give medical advice. Offer to have a dentist call them back.
    ```

    Also set the **greeting**, the first thing callers hear, like "Hi, thanks for calling Bright Smile Dental. How can I help?"
  </Step>

  <Step title="Knowledge and tools (optional for now)">
    **Knowledge** is what your agent knows: your price list, website or files. **Tools** are what it can do: book on your Google Calendar, save a lead to a Google Sheet, send a WhatsApp message, or transfer to a person.

    Skip these for your first test. See [Knowledge](/guides/knowledge) and [Tools](/guides/tools) when you're ready.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go live">
    In **Channels**, you choose where the agent works. You can leave it for now. Click **Go live** to save and publish your agent.

    Publishing matters: callers only ever hear the published version. Later edits stay a draft until you click **Publish changes**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Talk to it">
    Open the agent's **Test** tab and click **Start test call**. Allow the microphone when your browser asks. Your agent greets you. Talk to it like a customer would:

    - Ask your opening hours.
    - Try to book an appointment.
    - Interrupt it halfway through a sentence.
    - Switch language mid-sentence.

    Hang up when you're done. The test call appears in **Calls**, with its recording, transcript and a short summary.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Make it better

Most agents get good after two or three rounds of "test, read the transcript, fix the instructions". When the agent says something wrong, add a line to its instructions about that exact case, publish, and test again.
