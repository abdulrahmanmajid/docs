Kallabot measures phone and browser calls in **minutes**. Your workspace has a **pool** of minutes, and you can give some to each person on your team.

## Where minutes come from

<Steps>
  <Step title="The pool">
    Minutes your workspace has. Calls that don't belong to anyone in particular, like an agent answering your main number, use the pool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Each person's share">
    Give minutes to a person from **People**: open them and move minutes from the pool. Calls they make use their own minutes first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How a call uses minutes

- A call reserves a few minutes when it starts, so two calls can't spend the same minute.
- When it ends, it's charged for the time it actually took and the rest goes back.
- If a call was **transferred** to a person through Kallabot, the transferred part is charged at a separate rate. If your own phone company carried it, it isn't charged in Kallabot at all; the call page says which.
- Minutes settle shortly after a call ends. Until then, the call shows its minutes as an estimate.

## When minutes run out

When there are no minutes left for a call, Kallabot won't start it, and the person who tried sees why. Incoming calls aren't put through to the agent either. So keep an eye on your balance, especially before a busy week.

<Tip>
  Owners can see the workspace's minutes, each person's balance and how many minutes were used today.
</Tip>

## Add minutes

Buy minute packs, or switch on auto top-up so you never run out. See [Plans and billing](/guides/billing).
