Your agent can reply to messages on your **Facebook Page** (Messenger) and your **Instagram** business account, with the same knowledge, tools and tone as everywhere else.

## Connect

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Messenger">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the agent's Channels">Go to your agent, then **Channels → Messenger**.</Step>
      <Step title="Continue with Facebook">Sign in with the Facebook account that manages your Page, and allow access.</Step>
      <Step title="Pick your Page">Choose the Facebook Page the agent should answer for.</Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Instagram">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the agent's Channels">Go to your agent, then **Channels → Instagram**.</Step>
      <Step title="Continue with Instagram">Sign in on Instagram with your business or creator account. You don't need a Facebook Page.</Step>
      <Step title="Pick the account">Choose the account the agent should answer for.</Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

You can connect both, plus WhatsApp, to the same agent.

## What the agent does

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Replies to messages" icon="messages-square">
    Answers DMs and Page messages around the clock.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Comment to DM" icon="message-square-reply">
    When someone comments a keyword on your post (like "PRICE"), they get a private message with the answer. On Instagram, the agent can also post a short public reply.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Story mentions" icon="at-sign">
    When someone mentions you in their story, they get a thank-you message.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Hands over to you" icon="hand">
    When you reply from Instagram or Meta Business Suite yourself, the agent steps back in that chat for a while, so you never talk over each other.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The studio

Each connected account has a studio where you set:

- **Greeting** and **ice breakers**: the welcome message and the questions people can tap to start.
- **Menu**: shortcuts that stay in the chat.
- **Comment to DM** keywords and messages, and **story mention** replies.
- **Hours**, **hand-off** and anything the agent should do differently on this channel.

Click **Sync now** to send the greeting, ice breakers and menu to Meta. If the agent also has a WhatsApp number, people can tap **Continue on WhatsApp**.

## Share links

On the agent's **Channels** tab you'll find ready-made links and QR codes (`m.me`, `ig.me` and `wa.me`) to put on your website, flyers or shop window.

## Good to know

- Your team can reply to a Messenger or Instagram chat for up to **7 days** after the customer's last message.
- Photos and files the agent sends go as links for now.
- All chats are in your [Inbox](/guides/inbox), next to WhatsApp and web chat.
