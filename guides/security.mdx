## Your sign-in

You sign in with your email and a six-digit code we send you, or with a password if you've set one.

Under **Settings → Security** you can:

- See every device you're signed in on, and sign one out.
- **Sign out everywhere** at once, for example after losing a laptop.
- Read your recent sign-in history.

## Audit log

The **Audit log** records who did what in your workspace, and when: agents created and published, numbers bought and released, people invited, roles changed, API keys created and revoked, webhooks changed, and every time someone read a call transcript. Open **Audit log** in the app to see each entry with the person's name and face.

## Who can see conversations

Recordings, transcripts and chat messages are treated as private:

- Each role only sees what it needs. See [Roles](/guides/team#roles).
- Members see their own calls; managers see their branch.
- Reading a transcript is itself recorded in the audit log.
- Phone numbers show partly hidden (like `+97150•••4567`) unless a person is allowed to see call content.

## Do not call

Add numbers to the **Suppression list** and no agent will ever call them. If someone tells your agent to stop calling, honour it by adding them here.

## For developers

API keys are scoped, expire within a year, and can be revoked instantly. Webhooks are signed. See [Authentication](/developers/authentication) and [Webhooks](/developers/webhooks).
