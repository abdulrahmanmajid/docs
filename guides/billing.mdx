## The Brokerage trial

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the trial">No card needed. You get 30 minutes to try everything.</Step>
  <Step title="Add a card before it ends">Your Brokerage plan carries on when the trial ends, with its full monthly minutes.</Step>
  <Step title="Or let it end">If no card is added by the end of the 14 days, the trial cancels on its own. Nothing is charged.</Step>
</Steps>

## Buying minutes

- **Minute packs** come in 100, 500 or 2,000 minutes, all at one flat rate per minute.
- **Auto top-up** buys a pack for you when your balance runs low, so calls never stop. Choose when it kicks in and which pack it buys.
- **Extra seats** can be added to a Brokerage plan when your team grows.

Minutes go into your workspace's pool. From there you can share them out to your team. See [Minutes](/guides/minutes).

## Monthly or yearly

Pay for your plan monthly or yearly. On a yearly plan, your plan's minutes still arrive **every month**.

## Card and invoices

Your card, billing details and invoices are managed in Stripe's secure billing portal. Open it from **Billing** in the app. Who can see and change billing depends on their [role](/guides/team#roles).

<Info>
  Developers: billing is in the API too (plans, the billing summary, invoices, checkout links and auto top-up). See the **Billing** section of the [API reference](/api-reference/introduction).
</Info>
