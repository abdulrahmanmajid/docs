A webhook tells another system about something the moment it happens in Kallabot: a call ended, a summary is ready, a WhatsApp message arrived, an agent was published. Your developer, your CRM or a [workflow](/guides/workflows) receives it and acts on it.

Find them in **Settings → For developers → Webhooks**.

## Add an endpoint

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add endpoint">
    Paste the **Endpoint URL**: the web address that should receive events. It must start with `https://`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose events">
    Pick **Every event**, or **Choose events instead** and tick the ones you want, grouped as Calls, Chats, Tools, Phone numbers, Agents and Knowledge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose agents (optional)">
    Leave **Every agent**, or pick **Chosen agents** to only send events about some of them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide on content">
    Turn on **Include content** only if the receiving system needs phone numbers, message text or summaries. Leave it off otherwise.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the signing secret">
    Kallabot shows a **signing secret** once. Give it to your developer: it proves each event really came from Kallabot.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check it works

Click **Send test**. Kallabot sends a sample event straight away and shows what your server answered. If it says **Your server didn't take the test**, open the delivery to see the answer it got.

## The delivery log

**Deliveries** lists every event sent, with each attempt, and refreshes every 15 seconds. Open one to see exactly what was sent and what came back. Click **Retry** to send it again.

## When an endpoint fails

If your server doesn't answer, Kallabot keeps trying for about a day. If it keeps failing for **5 days**, the endpoint turns itself off and the workspace owners get an email. Nothing is lost: events are kept for 30 days.

To recover:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fix the receiving server">Make sure it's back up.</Step>
  <Step title="Turn the endpoint on">Click **Turn on**. Its failure count resets.</Step>
  <Step title="Replay what it missed">Click **Replay failed since…** and pick when the trouble started.</Step>
</Steps>

## Rotate the secret

If the signing secret may have leaked, click **Rotate secret**. The new secret is shown once. The old one keeps working for 24 hours, so your developer has time to switch.
