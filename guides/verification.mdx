Phone regulators in many countries want to know who's behind a number. Before you can buy a local number there, you send a **verification**: your business details, an address in the right place, and a document or two. Once it's approved, you can reuse it for more numbers in that country.

Kallabot tells you, before you buy, whether a number needs one and exactly what it asks for.

## Start a verification

You can start one from **Numbers → Add → Start a verification**, or right in the middle of buying a number that needs it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the country and number type">
    Requirements depend on both. A UK mobile number asks for different things than a UK local one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell us who you are">
    Your business name and details, or your own if you're a sole trader.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an address">
    Many countries want an address in that country, or even in the number's area. Save it once; your address book is reused next time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload documents">
    For example a trade licence, a utility bill or an ID. Kallabot says which ones count. Documents you've uploaded before are in your library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check and send">
    Kallabot checks your answers against the country's rules before you send, so you catch mistakes early. Then send it for review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After you send it

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Waiting** | The regulator's review is in progress. It can take from hours to a couple of weeks, depending on the country. |
| **Approved** | You're verified. Any number you saved to buy is bought automatically. |
| **Needs a fix** | Something was rejected. Kallabot shows you what, so you can correct it and send it again. |
| **Rules changed** | The country changed its rules. Update the verification to keep your numbers. |

Numbers you saved while waiting stay reserved for you where possible. If one gets taken in the meantime, you can swap it for another.

## Keeping it current

Some verifications need renewing, and you can replace one if your business details change. You'll see a prompt on the verification when either is needed.

<Info>
  Verification is reviewed by our phone number provider, Twilio, under the country's rules. Kallabot sends them only what the verification asks for.
</Info>
