**Calls** lists every phone and browser call, inbound and outbound, newest first. Live calls sit at the top.

## Find a call

Search by name, number or words from the conversation, or filter by agent, direction, outcome, date, length, mood or tag. Save a set of filters as a **view** (like "Missed calls this week") to come back to it in one click. Views can be shared with your team.

## What's in a call

Open any call to see:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Summary" icon="file-text">
    A few lines on what happened, the outcome (like "booked"), the caller's mood, and anything they asked for, such as a call back.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Recording" icon="audio-lines">
    Listen to the whole call, including any transfer or teammate who joined. Recording is on per agent.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Transcript" icon="captions">
    Every turn, word for word, with who said it and when.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Details the agent collected" icon="list-checks">
    The caller's name, email and anything else you asked the agent to note.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tool runs" icon="wrench">
    What the agent looked up or booked during the call, and what came back.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Timeline" icon="list">
    Ringing, answered, transfers, teammates joining, hang-up.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Add **notes** for your team, **tag** calls, and mark them **reviewed**. Calls that need a look (an upset caller, an unanswered question) are flagged for review.

## Make a call

Click **Make call**, enter the number, choose the agent and the number to call from, then:

- **Call now** to dial straight away. You can watch it live, or close the window and the call carries on.
- **Schedule call** to pick a time. See [Scheduled calls](/guides/scheduled-calls).

The agent opens with its outbound greeting. Tell it why it's calling in its instructions, so it can answer "Why are you calling me?".

## Export

Download the calls you've filtered as a CSV file for a spreadsheet or your CRM.

## Who sees what

What each person can see depends on their [role](/guides/team#roles): members see their own calls, managers see their branch, and owners and admins see everything. Recordings and transcripts are kept from the billing and viewer roles. Every time someone reads a transcript, it's noted in the [audit log](/guides/security#audit-log).

## Block a number

Add numbers you must never call to your **suppression list**. Agents will refuse to call them, even if someone tries.
