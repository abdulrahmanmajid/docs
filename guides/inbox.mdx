The inbox is where every chat your agents handle comes together: **WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and web chat**. Open it from **Agents → Conversations**. (Phone calls have their own page, [Calls](/guides/calls).)

## Read along

Each conversation shows the whole chat, who the customer is, which agent is answering, and a short summary at the top, so you can catch up in seconds. Unread chats are marked, and the tabs show what's open, what needs you, and what's yours.

## Take over and hand back

<Steps>
  <Step title="Take over">
    Click **Take over** on any chat. The agent stops replying in that chat, and you type to the customer yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reply">
    Write your message, or insert a **quick reply** (a saved answer) with its shortcut. You can send photos and files too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hand back">
    When you're done, click **Hand back** and the agent carries on, with everything you said in its memory of the chat.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The agent hands chats to you as well: when a customer asks for a person, or something matches your hand-off rules. Those chats show up under **Needs you**.

<Note>
  WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger only allow replies for a while after the customer's last message (24 hours on WhatsApp, up to 7 days for a teammate on Instagram and Messenger). Each chat shows how long you have. Web chat has no limit.
</Note>

## Keep on top of it

| Tool | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Done** | Mark a chat you've dealt with, so it leaves your open list. |
| **Snooze** | Hide a chat until a time you pick. |
| **Owner** | Assign a chat to someone on your team. |
| **Priority and tags** | Mark what's urgent, and label chats (like "lead" or "complaint"). |
| **Notes** | Private notes for your team. Customers never see them. |
| **Summary** | Ask for a fresh summary of a long chat. |
| **Customer history** | The same customer's earlier calls and chats, in one list. |

## Bookings

Switch to the **Bookings** view to see every appointment your agents booked from chats, by day.

## Inbox settings

Choose how new chats are shared among your team: in turns, left unassigned, or to whoever handled that customer last. You can also pass a chat on automatically if its owner doesn't reply within a few minutes.
